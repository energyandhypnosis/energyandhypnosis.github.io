Melissa’s Mission

Through witnessing countless people feeling confused, stuck on strong medications, and lost in outdated diagnoses while working in the mental health sector — Melissa felt deep down that there must be better solutions to the challenges people experience, rather than a lifelong diagnosis and a prescription for symptom management.

Melissa left everything behind in Canada to travel and experience ancient spiritual practices and sacred psychoenergetic healing modalities across the world. While there, she discovered the interplay between neuroscience, biology, spirituality, and consciousness. Later in her travels, she found hypnosis and the massive impact it had on others, and devoted herself to sharing the wisdom and tools she acquired.

Melissa does not use a diagnostic model; she sees every symptom through an energetic, emotional, and spiritual model. She sees everyone she works with as a pure light. With this, any stress, negative feeling, or habit is not someone’s true nature and can therefore be relieved with the right tools and understanding. Melissa has a way to help you step into your next level — no matter where you are in your journey.
